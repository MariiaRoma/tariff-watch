What the app does

Tariff Watch provides information about tariff rates between Canada and the US, based on publicly available sources (Finance Canada, Federal Register / USITC). The app lets you track selected HS codes and receive push notifications about rate changes.

Important: this is not legal or customs advice. Data is provided "as is", for informational purposes. Before making business decisions based on tariff rates (customs clearance, pricing, contracts), verify the data against official sources or consult a licensed customs broker or lawyer. We do not guarantee the completeness, accuracy, or timeliness of the data.

Data accuracy

Data is automatically synced daily from official sources: the Canadian side (tariffs on US goods) directly from the Finance Canada website, and the US side (tariffs on Canadian goods) via the official USITC HTS API. If a source is temporarily unavailable or has changed its page structure, the app keeps the previous data unchanged rather than overwriting it incorrectly — so there may be delays between an official rate change and its appearance in the app, especially if a data source is unstable. Always verify critical decisions directly against the original source.
