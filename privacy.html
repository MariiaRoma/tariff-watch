Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Tariff Watch ("the app", "we") is a PWA app that tracks tariff rate changes between Canada and the US. This page describes what data we collect and how we use it.

What data we collect

At this stage, the app collects a minimal amount of data:

  • Push subscription — if you enable notifications, your browser creates a technical "endpoint" (an address for delivering push messages) and encryption keys. We store these so we can send you notifications about rate changes for the HS codes on your watchlist.
  • Watchlist (list of HS codes) — the codes you add to track are stored locally in your browser (localStorage). If you enable push notifications, a copy of this list is also sent to our server along with your subscription's technical details — this is needed so we know which codes to notify you about.
  • Technical logs — standard serverless function logs (Netlify Functions) may temporarily contain the request's IP address as part of normal hosting operation.

We do not collect names, mailing addresses, payment information, or any other personal data at this stage — the app does not have an account system.

How we use data

The sole purpose of the data we collect is to deliver push notifications about tariff rate changes for the codes you track. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Analytics and third-party services

At this stage, the app does not use any third-party analytics services or advertising trackers.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete your push subscription at any time by turning off notifications in the app or in your browser settings. This removes the corresponding record from our server.

To request full deletion of all data associated with your device, contact us (details below).

Jurisdiction

The app is developed and based in the province of Manitoba, Canada (business registration is currently in progress), and processes user data in both Canada and the US. We aim to follow the principles of the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) regarding data minimization and transparency of use — Manitoba does not have a separate provincial private-sector privacy law, so PIPEDA is the primary applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The date of the most recent update is shown at the top of this page.
