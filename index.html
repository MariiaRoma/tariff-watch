About this data

Estimates only — not customs advice. Tariff Watch is not a licensed customs broker in Canada or the United States, and nothing here is a binding classification or ruling. Confirm any real shipment against the official CBSA / CBP schedules, or with a licensed customs broker, before you price or file it.

What's in this MVP

This build ships a curated sample of roughly 75 tariff lines, not the full official lists (874 items on the Canadian counter-tariff list; hundreds more across the three US Section 338 proclamations). It's meant to demonstrate the watchlist → alert → calculate loop, not to replace the primary sources below.

US → Canada direction data

The "US → CA" entries (Canada's counter-tariffs on US-origin goods) are copied at the HS10 level directly from the Department of Finance Canada's official published list, effective September 8, 2026.

Canada → US direction data

The "CA → US" entries (US Section 338 and Section 232 tariffs on Canadian-origin goods) are illustrative, category-level entries built from public reporting on the underlying proclamations. The White House had not published a browsable line-by-line list at the time this sample was built — treat these as a starting point for your own USITC HTS lookup, not a citable classification.

Path to real alerts

This static build simulates an alert by comparing your watchlist against a snapshot saved in your browser on your last visit — it can only catch changes between the data baked into two different builds of the app. A production version would replace the static data.js with a nightly sync job against the free USITC HTS API and the CBSA Customs Tariff download, diff the results, and push real notifications via the Web Push API (or a service like OneSignal) when a watched code changes.
